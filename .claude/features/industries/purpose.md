# Industry Solutions: Purpose

Industry landing pages deliver tailored solutions per vertical. Ordering below is the
site-wide ordering — it matches `lib/data/nav.ts`, the footer, and the home page
industries grid, and they must stay in sync.

### Co-primary markets — equal weight (see invariants.md #3)

1. **Real Estate** (`/industries/real-estate`) — boutique agencies of 1–10 agents, 24/7 showing booking, LPMAMA buyer qualification, Fair Housing compliance, Follow Up Boss/KVCore/EspoCRM.
2. **Pest Control** (`/industries/pest-control`) — PCOs, recurring quarterly subscription upsells, FieldRoutes/PestPac/GorillaDesk.

### Supported secondary verticals — never framed as a main focus

3. **HVAC** (`/industries/hvac`) — Heatwave emergency triage, $8,500 replacement leads, ServiceTitan calendar booking.
4. **Plumbing** (`/industries/plumbing`) — 2 AM burst pipe & flooding triage, main water valve shut-off advice, ServiceTitan booking.
5. **Roofing** (`/industries/roofing`) — Post-hail storm surge call handling, instant damage photo upload links, insurance claim intake.
6. **Electrical** (`/industries/electrical`) — 11:15 PM arcing breaker & power outage triage, main breaker safety guidance, 200A panel upgrades.

The two co-primary verticals are deliberately the first two entries in the site-wide
ordering, so a PCO or a broker sees themselves in the first row of the industries grid.
