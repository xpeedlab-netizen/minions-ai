# Minions AI — Crew Mascot Design Brief (3D Character Spec)

**Version:** 1.0 (Locked for WO#04 Pilot & WO#04.1 Fleet Rollout)  
**Style Target:** 3D Realistic Character Mascot (Pixar / Illumination-adjacent character realism, 3D studio render with physical materials, soft lighting, and depth of field).

---

## 1. Overall Build & Proportions
- **Character Class:** Stylized-but-grounded digital assistant capsule robot.
- **Proportions:** 1:1.2 head-to-body ratio. Friendly, approachable, squat proportions with a rounded-square ("squircle") head and a compact capsule torso.
- **Limbs & Joints:** Short, rounded articulated arms with 3-finger soft-grip hands. Sturdy, low-center-of-gravity pill legs.
- **Shared Chassis:** All 5 crew members share the exact same structural body frame and proportions, ensuring they read instantly as a single cohesive "Front Office Crew" unit.

---

## 2. Material & Texture Language
- **Body Shell:** Matte warm-white satin composite ceramic shell (subtle subsurface scattering, non-reflective, soft-touch feel).
- **Face & Visor:** Curved glossy black glass visor with soft internal LED eye glow and smooth specular highlights.
- **Joints & Internal Hardware:** Brushed anodized dark aluminum at shoulder, neck, and hip pivot points.
- **Trim & Accents:** High-durability matte silicone rubber bumpers in character accent colors.

---

## 3. Color System & Palette Harmonization
Designed to pair seamlessly with the website's design system (`bg-ink #0A1118`, `bg-cream #F7F4F0`, `teal #0E5C63`, `coral #C4472A`):

- **Shared Core:** Light warm-cream composite body shell across all 5 characters for fleet unity.
- **Character Primary Accents:**
  - **Rex (Voice Agent):** Deep Teal (`#0E5C63`) ear pads, headset trim, and glowing cyan visor eyes.
  - **Zip (Speed-to-Lead):** Vibrant Coral-Red (`#C4472A`) speed fins, trim, and amber-orange glow.
  - **Pip (Support AI):** Soft Blue (`#3A6EA5`) ear caps, chest trim, and calm sky-blue glow.
  - **Gia (CRM Automation):** Emerald Green (`#1B8A5A`) side clips, pipeline trim, and crisp mint glow.
  - **Otto (Back-Office):** Dark Slate Navy (`#2C3E50`) body collar, subtle silver trim, and warm white glow.

---

## 4. Shared Silhouette & Facial Expression
- **Head Unit:** Smooth squircle helmet featuring twin rounded top node caps.
- **Visor Expression:** Two soft-glowing capsule LED eyes that convey emotion (focused, friendly, attentive).
- **Chest Unit:** Recessed illuminated circular crew emblem on the upper chest.

---

## 5. Character-Specific Props & Function Styling
- **Rex (Voice Agent — WO#04 Focus):** Fitted with a sleek professional workstation headset with a flexible boom microphone. Poses include:
  - *Idle:* Standing confidently, headset resting comfortably, hand on hip or welcoming gesture.
  - *Active:* Touching headset earpiece or holding a glowing modern phone handset node with active soundwave ring indicators.
- **Zip (Speed-to-Lead — WO#04.1):** Aerodynamic ear fins, holding a glowing lightning message tablet.
- **Pip (Support AI — WO#04.1):** Dual support headset cups, holding a friendly floating chat message bubble.
- **Gia (CRM Automation — WO#04.1):** Organizational badge, holding a floating interactive pipeline column node.
- **Otto (Back-Office — WO#04.1):** Quiet scanner visor, holding a neat stack of glowing organized document cards.

---

## 6. Rendering & Lighting Guidelines
- **Lighting:** Three-point studio lighting (key light from top-left, soft warm fill, subtle rim light matching accent color).
- **Background:** Clean solid neutral grey (`#1A232A` or isolated PNG transparent) for effortless composite overlay onto homepage containers.
- **Camera:** Eye-level 85mm lens perspective, slight forced perspective for character presence without distortion.
